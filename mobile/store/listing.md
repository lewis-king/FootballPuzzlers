## Full description

A football trivia game in the spirit of guess who. Each question is a player telling you a bit of their story, and you have to work out who it is.

328 players across four packs:
* The Starter Pack: 90 Premier League questions
* The 2020s (new): 116 players from the last few years, from Lamine Yamal to Teemu Pukki
* World Cup: 61 questions
* Champions League: 61 questions

The facts come from the player's career, including things like:
* Clubs they've played for
* Teammates they've played with
* Managers they've played under
* Honours they've won, for club and country, and the odd moment you'd forgotten

Stuck? Each player has six yes-or-no clues (goalkeeper, defender, midfielder, forward, retired, English) and you get to pick two. You can also reveal the first few letters of the surname.

A bit of background around building the game:
You may or may not be part of a social circle that loves to talk about football, whether it's on social media or together at the pub. Every now and then someone in the group hears an intriguing, difficult question and the whole group tries to guess the answer. The guessing can go on for hours, depending on how hard the question is and what the group knows.
This app was built to bring that experience to an actual platform rather than an email thread or a group chat. There are no passes: the idea is to answer the question, and if you can't, get your friends involved.

The questions get harder as you go, and the game isn't designed to blitz through in one sitting.

Played the original app? Your progress carries over. A save code also lets you move it to a new phone.

I hope you enjoy it, and feel free to leave some feedback at hello@footballwhoami.com.
