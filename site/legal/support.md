# Support

Email hello@footballwhoami.com and I'll get back to you, usually within a couple of days.

## Moving to a new phone

Open Settings in the app and copy your save code. On the new phone, install the app, go to Settings, tap Enter a code and paste it in. Your progress comes with you.

## Getting your packs back

Packs you've bought are tied to your App Store or Google Play account. On a new phone, open Settings and tap Restore purchases.

## Coming back from the old version

If you played the original app, your progress and purchases come across automatically when you update. If something's missing, email me with roughly how far you'd got.

## Found a wrong answer?

Football moves fast. Email me the question and what you think is wrong, and I'll check it for the next update.

## New pack alerts

Turn them on or off in Settings. You'll only ever get a notification when a new pack comes out.
