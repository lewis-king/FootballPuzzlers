# Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Football Who Am I? is a quiz app made by Lewis King ("I", "me"). This policy explains what the app and this website collect, and why. The short version: there are no accounts, your progress stays on your phone, and the only outside companies involved are Google (ads), Apple and Google (payments and notifications) and Expo (sending notifications).

## Your progress

Your answers, clues, revealed letters and streak are stored on your phone only. They're never sent to me. The save code in Settings is made on your phone from that progress. It only goes anywhere if you copy it and paste it somewhere yourself.

## Ads

The free packs are paid for by ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, rough location and how you interact with ads. In the UK and EEA the app asks for your consent first, using Google's consent form, and you can change your choice later. On iPhone, Apple's tracking prompt decides whether ads can use your advertising identifier. If you say no, you still see ads, but they're less tailored. Google's use of this data is covered by its own policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## Purchases

Packs are bought through the App Store or Google Play. They handle the payment and your payment details, and I never see them. The app only learns that a pack was bought, so it can unlock it.

## New-pack alerts

If you tap "Notify me" or switch on New pack alerts, your phone gives the app a push token: a random identifier that lets a notification reach this app on this phone. It's stored on a server I run on Cloudflare, with your phone's platform (iPhone or Android) and the date it was last seen, and it's used for one thing only: telling you when a new pack is out. Notifications are delivered through Expo (expo.dev) and Apple's or Google's notification services. Turning alerts off in Settings deletes the token. Tokens for phones that no longer have the app are deleted the next time a notification goes out.

## Emails to support

If you email me, I'll use your email address and what you send only to reply to you. Email is handled by Cloudflare Email Routing, which forwards it to my Gmail (Google).

## This website

This site uses no cookies and no advertising trackers. To see how the page is used, it counts visits and a few anonymous steps (reaching each part of the page, trying the sample question, tapping a store button) with Cloudflare Web Analytics and Cloudflare's own event counts. These record the step, your country and the type of device, and nothing that identifies you: no IP address, no cookie and no id. They aren't shared with anyone. The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which keeps short-lived server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

## Children

The app is a general-audience football quiz and isn't aimed at children under 13.

## Your rights

You can ask me what I hold about you, or ask me to delete it, by emailing hello@footballwhoami.com. In practice, the only thing I can hold is a push token, if you switched alerts on, and any emails you've sent me. If you're in the UK you can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

## Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update it here and change the date at the top.
